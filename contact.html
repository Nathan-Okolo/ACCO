Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with A-COO Edu Consults?
Simply fill out our contact form above or reach out via WhatsApp. We'll schedule a free initial consultation to understand your needs and recommend the best path forward.
Do you charge fees for admissions assistance?
Our admissions service to UK universities is provided at zero charge to the student. We are compensated by the institutions we partner with, meaning you get expert guidance at no cost to you.
Which countries do you serve?
We primarily serve clients in Nigeria, the United Kingdom, and The Gambia. However, we work with international students from around the world seeking admissions to UK and Nigerian universities.
How long does the admissions process take?
Timelines vary depending on the institution and intake period. We have successfully secured admissions in as little as one week and CAS letters within three days for qualified candidates.
Can you help with research even if I'm not a student?
Absolutely. Our research and analysis services are available to students, academics, NGOs, government agencies, and private businesses. Get in touch to discuss your specific research needs.
