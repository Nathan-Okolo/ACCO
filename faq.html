General Questions

We are an experienced and licensed educational and business consultancy with a speciality in research, local and international studies, educational content and services including materials, visas and training, business management, advisory, assessments and evaluation as well as accounting and marketing services.
Our destinations and areas of coverage include Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, the United States (US), Australia, the United Kingdom (UK), Canada, and the Netherlands.
Our services are both face-to-face and virtual. We offer consultation and assistance for all services and provide 24/7 customer service as well as chatbot assistance on our website at www.acooconsult.com.
All services relating to international studies applications are FREE, except application fees charged by some schools in the US and Canada.

Admissions & International Studies

We provide a tailored career and university choice guide based on your individual situation. We recommend courses and institutions based on proximity, client preferences, suitability, conditions, demands, and academic prospects. Services include a review of:

  1. CV and Resume
  2. Statement of Purpose (SOP) and Personal Statement (PS)
  3. Form fillings
  4. Application submission
  5. Document review, scanning and uploads
  6. Gathering and validation of admission and visa documents
  7. Scholarship advisory and funding counselling
Yes. We provide guidance on the entire student visa process, including document preparation, submission, accommodation searches, travel planning, and other pre-study preparations to ensure a smooth transition.
Timelines vary depending on the institution and programme. We have achieved admissions in as little as one week and CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) in three days for urgent cases. Contact us early to maximise your options.
