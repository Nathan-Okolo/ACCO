Research Services

Research & Research Analyses

Our team of expert researchers and consultants deliver high-quality academic, organisational, and policy research. We serve students, institutions, NGOs, and government bodies with zero-plagiarism, rigorous, and impactful research outputs.

Our establishment is the product of years of research and in-depth inquiry to provide educational solutions to businesses, schools, individuals, and organisations. We have access to world-class partners and leading consultancies in the UK, US, and Nigeria.

Our landmark achievements include research at Taraba State University, Michael Okpara University, Alex Ekwueme Federal University Ndufu-Alike (AE-FUNAI), Ebonyi State University (EBSU), and Baze University. We have internationally trained researchers from Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, the US, the UK, and Australia.

Our delivery to the government and NGOs in Northern Nigeria is outstanding. In 2017 and 2018, we secured over 200 million naira worth of grants for over 40 NGOs and effectively managed many governmental projects from start to finish. Partners include DI Rescue House, International Institute for Gender and Development (IIFGD), Support for Gender and Development Network (SGDN), and International Centre for Gender and Development (ICGD).

Contact us for professional, one-to-one research assistance tailored to your project needs.

Our Research Services Include:

  1. Undergraduate projects and research
  2. Postgraduate research and projects
  3. Research proposals and grant writing
  4. Dissertations and theses
  5. Organisational research and analyses
  6. Research training and tutorials
  7. Qualitative, quantitative, and thematic analyses
